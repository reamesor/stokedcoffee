Story

Coffee, like life, isn’t meant to stay.

Stoked started with Nofah, in Bulacan. But Bulacan wasn’t where it was meant to stay.

Somewhere along the way, Maerianne came in — and Stoked became a partnership, equal in every way that matters. Same investment, same stake, same “we.”

What’s split is the work: Nofah on the ground, pouring and setting up wherever they land. Maerianne behind the scenes — the site, the posters, the story, the people who make the next pop-up possible.

Different jobs, one coffee shop. Coffee, like life, isn’t meant to stay in one place.

Nofah — Co-founder & operations lead, head of brew operations

Maerianne — Co-founder & creative director, partnerships
